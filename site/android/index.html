Authentic Android experience

Local security, shown clearly.

Every interface shown below comes from the real Android app running with a reviewed synthetic vault. The campaign adds presentation and context, never invented controls.

Encrypted local vault

OTP seeds remain protected locally and do not require an OTP Harbor cloud account.

Camera QR import

Scan standard account QR codes and Google Authenticator transfer QR codes directly.

Three unlock choices

Choose strong biometrics, the Android device screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password), or the master password. The master password always remains available as recovery.

Encrypted portability

Create password-protected backups compatible with supported OTP Harbor platforms.

Focused mobile actions

Copy codes and manage accounts through touch-oriented actions designed for smaller screens.

Auditable releases

GitHub APKs use the permanent package ID io.github.legends.otpharbor and a pinned signing certificate verified by release automation.

Touch-first workflow

Know every gesture and security choice.

Tap to copy. Swipe right for QR and edit, or left for confirmed deletion. Scan standard account and Google Authenticator transfer QR codes through the system camera, and show an account QR only when moving it to a trusted authenticator.

The complete guide also explains encrypted backup conflict resolution, biometric and device-screen-lock recovery, the 30-second background grace period, and the explicit app-lock opt-out.

One product, multiple platforms

Move without giving up control.

OTP Harbor keeps Android and desktop within one product and security model. Each platform is distributed as its own native package; the Android APK is never bundled into a desktop installer.

Before moving accounts, create and test an encrypted backup. QR codes, OTP seeds, generated codes and backup passwords are sensitive information.
